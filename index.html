🍰Breeding, explained like you're 5

The short version — everything you need to hatch your first eggs.
  1. Build a Breeding Farm (Technology level 19). Also build an Egg Incubator (level 7, needs Paldium).
  2. Put one boy pal and one girl pal in the farm. Any two species can make a baby together — genders just have to be different. Check a pal's gender in your Palbox.
  3. Bake a Cake and put it in the little chest attached to the farm. No cake, no baby. One cake = one egg.
    5× Flour8× Red Berries7× Milk8× Eggs2× Honey
  4. Wait about 5 minutes. An egg pops out. Stick it in the incubator and wait for it to hatch (bigger and rarer eggs take longer).
  5. Which pal comes out? Three simple rules:
    • Same species parents → same species baby. Lamball + Lamball = Lamball.
    • Different species → the game does secret math (see the deep dive). Just use the Breed tab — it tells you exactly what you'll get.
    • A few pairs are special recipes that ignore the math — they're marked with a gold UNIQUE badge in this app. Example: Relaxaurus + Sparkit = Relaxaurus Lux.
  6. Why bother? Babies can inherit passive skills from both parents. Breed a pal with great passives into a strong species and you get a super-pal you could never catch in the wild.
Legendary pals (Jetragon, Frostallion, Paladius, Necromus…) can't be created by mixing other pals — the only way to breed one is two of the same legendary. Their entries say "unique only" in the Paldex.

🧬Deep dive

How the game actually decides what hatches — the same rules this calculator runs.
The species formula (breeding power)

Every species has a hidden number called breeding power (internally CombiRank — you can see it for every pal in the Paldex tab). When two different species breed:

target = floor((powerA + powerB + 1) ÷ 2)

The child is the species whose breeding power is closest to that target. If two species are exactly equally close, a hidden priority value decides the winner. That's it — the whole species system is one averaging formula.

  • Not every pal can be a "math result": legendaries and all sub-species (Relaxaurus Lux, Incineram Noct, etc.) are excluded from the pool — only 183 of the 288 species can hatch from averaging. The excluded ones need their unique recipe or same-species parents.
  • Unique recipes override the math. This app checks them first, exactly like the game.
  • A handful of recipes are gender-dependent: Katress ♀ × Wixen ♂ gives Katress Ignis, but Katress ♂ × Wixen ♀ gives Wixen Noct. The Breed tab shows both outcomes when it matters.
  • Because it's an average, you can often reach the same child from hundreds of different pairs — that's what the Find Parents tab enumerates.
Passive skill inheritance (the real endgame)

The child can inherit up to 4 passive skills drawn from the combined pool of both parents' passives, and it can also roll random passives that neither parent has.

  • Roughly speaking (community-measured, the game doesn't show the odds): if the parents together have exactly the 4 passives you want and nothing else, about 1 in 10 eggs is a perfect 4/4 baby.
  • Every junk passive on either parent dilutes the pool and tanks your odds. The meta strategy is to breed "clean" parents first: pals whose only passives are ones you want.
  • Classic workflow: combine 2 desired passives on one parent, 2 on the other → hatch until a child has 3–4 → use that child as a new parent. Passives have no gender, so any keeper can pair with any opposite-gender partner of the right species chain.
  • Popular combat set: Legend (from legendaries — yes, it inherits!), Musclehead, Ferocious, element +20%. Work set: Artisan, Serious, Lucky, Work Slave.
IVs, genders and stats
  • Each pal has hidden IVs (0–100) for HP, Attack and Defense. A child's IVs are usually inherited from one parent or the other, with a chance to roll fresh — so hatch several keepers and compare. (IV viewer mods or the in-game stat comparison help here.)
  • Gender is rolled per species — every pal card in this app shows the ♂/♀ odds. Some species are heavily skewed (useful to know when you need a specific gender for a gender-locked recipe or your next pairing).
  • Alpha (oversized) status does not inherit; the Lucky passive from lucky pals inherits like any other passive.
Eggs, incubation and throughput
  • Egg type and size depend on the child (element + rarity). Bigger/rarer eggs take longer — huge eggs can take many real-time hours at default settings.
  • Eggs have a temperature preference ("feels a little cold…"). Match it — put the incubator near a heater or campfire for warm-loving eggs, a cooler for cold-loving ones — and incubation speeds up significantly.
  • Playing on your own server? The world setting PalEggDefaultHatchingTime (default 72 h for huge eggs) scales all incubation — many servers set it to 0–2 h.
  • One farm produces one egg per cake per ~5 minutes while both parents are assigned and fed. Build multiple breeding farms to parallelize.
  • Cakes stored in the breeding farm's chest never spoil — stock it up.
